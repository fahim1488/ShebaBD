# ShebaBD Frontend Design System & Color Tokens

## Palette
- **Forest Ink (Dark Background)**: `#0F172A` / `#022c22`
- **Primary Emerald**: `#10B981` (Accent and Actions)
- **Secondary Sky**: `#0EA5E9` (Interactive badges)
- **Crimson Red**: `#EF4444` (Blood donations & Emergency alerts)
- **Warm Gold**: `#F59E0B` (Featured campaigns & Warnings)

## Typography
- Headings: Inter / Outfit, Sans-Serif
- Numeric & Badges: IBM Plex Mono
